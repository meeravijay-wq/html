honeybees life

the honey bee [apis mellifera] lives in colonies where the members of the hive are divided into three parts. the queen is the captain its purpose is to lay eggs to create the next generation of bees.

the male honeybee purpose is to mate with the queen, several hundred bees live in each hive during spring

honeybees produce honey as much as food stores in winter, honeybees produce three times then how much they need if the queen bee dies workers will create a new queen by selecting a young larvae they feed a special food called royal jelly.

facts about honeybees

honeybees fly at warp speed, reaching approxiametely 25 km/h and beating their wings two hundred times per second each body has 170 odarant recepetors which means they have only one sense of smell. they use this within the hives and to recognise diffrent flowers when finding food.

the average worker bee works to 5 to 6 weeks during this time they produce twelfth of a tablespoon of honey
